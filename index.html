What this is about

Child abuse reporting reform that recognizes Child Psychological Abuse as named, defined, reportable, and preventable.

Problem

Pathogenic parenting and manipulation cause recognizable harm to a child’s personality and development, often missed or mislabeled in systems built for physical or sexual abuse.

Reform

We seek statutory clarity so psychological abuse is named precisely, reportable by professionals and the public, and actionable by the judiciary—without “scoring” abuse.

Principle

We cannot heal what we cannot name. The attachment system of the brain governs love, bonding, grief, and loss. Identifying harm there is essential to protect development.
